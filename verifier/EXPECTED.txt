verifier/EXPECTED.txt -- expected outputs of verify_all.sh
==================================================================================================================

verify_all.sh prints one line per step, "PASS  STEP  (SECONDS s)" or "FAIL  STEP ...", and ends with
"ALL CHECKS PASSED" (exit status 0). A step passes when its program exits with status 0 and its log
(work/logs/STEP.log) contains the strings below (fixed-string match; lines starting with "!" must NOT occur).
The exact values are those of the paper. Times: measured on one core of an AMD Ryzen 7 5800H (--threads 1),
except where marked (the times of parts (h), (i), (j) and the total come from the test of the second version, with
--threads 2 on the same machine); they will differ on other machines. Peak memory of every step is below 500 MB (the
largest are the rv2_dual.py steps of part (i), up to about 460 MB). Parts (h), (i) and (j) were added in the second
version of the package; their expected values come from the independent reviews of the catalogue and of the dual
points (and, for (j), from the definitions), never from the producer's outputs.
