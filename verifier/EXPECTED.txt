verifier/EXPECTED.txt -- expected outputs of verify_all.sh
==================================================================================================================

verify_all.sh prints one line per step, "PASS  STEP  (SECONDS s)" or "FAIL  STEP ...", and ends with
"ALL CHECKS PASSED" (exit status 0). A step passes when its program exits with status 0 and its log
(work/logs/STEP.log) contains the strings below (fixed-string match; lines starting with "!" must NOT occur).
The exact values are those of the paper. Times: measured on one core of an AMD Ryzen 7 5800H (--threads 1),
except where marked (the times of parts (h) to (l) and the total come from the tests of the second and third
versions, with --threads 2 on the same machine); they will differ on other machines. Peak memory of every step is
below 600 MB (the largest are stab_cf, about 585 MB, sigma_prep, about 475 MB, and the rv2_dual.py steps of part (i),
up to about 460 MB), except the prep steps of --raw sigcat_J4 (about 1.5 GB) and --raw sigcat_K5lt (about 0.85 GB).
Parts (h), (i) and (j) were added in the second version of the package, parts (k) and (l) in the third; their
expected values come from the independent reviews of the catalogue, of the dual points, of the sigma certificate and
of the stability results (and, for (j), from the definitions), never from the producer's outputs. In the fourth
version the certificates of Theorem main (b), (d), (e) (K6_4, K5_4minus, K6_4minus) were replaced by larger ones;
their expected values in (a) come from their independent review R7_B2 (its own runs of the same checkers), and the
values of (i) that refer to them (b, ceil12(1 - b), V - b) from the same review. Parts (m), (n) and (o) were added in
the fifth version, and the sixth added seven certificates to part (m); their expected values come from the independent
reviews of these files (R7_SIG7 with its addendum A1, R7_SIG8 with its addenda A1 and A2, R7_J4S1, R7_J4S2) and from
the definitions, except those of sigcat_keys and sigcat_keys_neg (a producer program checking the key lists; its log)
and of the optional part (o) (the producer's own checkers; their logs). Part (p) was added in the seventh version;
its expected values come from the independent reviews R7_T3b, R7_F2 and R7_P6 (their logs).
